# HDFC AMC Financial Analysis
**Prepared by:** Tushar Narendra Varkhede

## Financial Performance, Business Drivers & Relative Valuation Analysis of HDFC Asset Management Company

**Analysis Period:** FY2022–FY2026

## Project Overview

This project analyzes HDFC Asset Management Company across five financial years, focusing on:

- Financial performance and revenue growth
- PAT growth and profitability
- Business drivers such as AUM and investor growth
- Operating cash flow and financial strength
- Peer comparison
- Relative valuation using P/E and P/B multiples

## Key Areas Covered

1. Executive Summary
2. Raw Data
3. Business Drivers
4. Growth Analysis
5. Profitability
6. Cash Flow & Financial Strength
7. Peer Comparison
8. Valuation
9. Insights

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Financial statement analysis
- Ratio analysis
- CAGR and year-over-year growth analysis
- Peer comparison
- Relative valuation

## Important Limitations

- FY2021 is not included as a separate project year.
- FY22 ROE and ROA are shown as N/A where the required opening-balance data is not included.
- FY26 capital expenditure is marked as Not Verified.
- FY26 free cash flow is shown as N/A because FY26 capital expenditure has not been verified.
- Valuation data is based on the workbook snapshot dated 15 September 2026 and may change with market prices.
